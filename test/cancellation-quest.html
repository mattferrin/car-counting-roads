How it works: every tap cancels the same amount from both sides — hundreds first, then tens, then ones, one unit at a time. The difference never changes, so when the second side reaches zero, what is left on the first side is the answer.

Voices: after each move only the place that just changed is counted — tens while tens are being cancelled (“seven tens”, “six tens”…), then ones on their own (“three ones”, “two ones”…). The full count is always on screen, and the answer is spoken in full (“The answer is five tens, seven ones!”). The two voices (one per side) take turns. Sound starts after the first tap. You can also open a problem directly, e.g. cancellation-quest.html?theme=mermaids&a=84&b=27
