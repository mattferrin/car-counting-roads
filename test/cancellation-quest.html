How it works: every tap cancels the same amount from both sides — hundreds first, then tens, then ones, one unit at a time. The difference never changes, so when the second side reaches zero, what is left on the first side is the answer.

Voices: counts are spoken in tens and ones (“eight tens, four ones”), not fused names like “eighty-four”, so the counting pattern stays visible: six tens, five tens, four tens… then ones count down on their own. The two voices (one per side) take turns. Sound starts after the first tap. You can also open a problem directly, e.g. cancellation-quest.html?theme=mermaids&a=84&b=27
