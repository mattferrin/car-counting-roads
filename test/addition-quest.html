How it works: every move transfers from the smaller side to the bigger side — complete tens first, then ones, one unit at a time. The total never changes, so when the smaller side reaches zero, what is left on the bigger side is the answer. The panels keep the order of the problem (first number on the left, second on the right), so the move goes left-to-right or right-to-left depending on which side is smaller.

Voices: after each move only the place that just changed is counted — tens while tens are moving, then ones on their own. The full count is always on screen, and the answer is spoken in full (“The answer is seven tens, three ones!”). The two voices (one per side) take turns. Sound starts after the first tap. You can also open a problem directly, e.g. addition-quest.html?theme=mermaids&a=47&b=26
